Privacy for Freezeline

Effective 11 November 2026 · iPhone version

Freezeline is a photography puzzle game developed by Trinath Sai Subhash Reddy Pittala. This policy explains the game’s local saves, optional Apple services, advertising and information you choose to share.

Your game stays on your device

Progress, photographs made in the virtual park, favorite prints, winning paths, character names, Puzzle Lab creations and preferences are stored on your device. Freezeline does not send your save or custom gameplay diagnostics to a developer-operated server. Local checkpoints and recovery copies are also stored on that device.

Game Center and iCloud

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple provides the app with your game-scoped player identifier and nickname. The game can report earned achievements and leaderboard scores to Apple. Game Center recognition is separate from the saved progress on your device.

You can choose to create iCloud backups through Apple’s saved-game service. These snapshots include progress, virtual photographs, saved paths and Puzzle Lab data. They use your iCloud storage and require iCloud Drive and Game Center. A backup is restored only when you choose it and confirm the replacement. We do not receive a copy on a developer-operated server. Apple’s account settings and privacy policy govern its services.

Advertising and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob. It may offer optional rewarded ads and occasional ads at natural breaks after completed puzzles. TestFlight and development builds use test inventory.

Google’s advertising software may process network information such as an IP-derived general region; device or app-scoped identifiers where permitted; ad views and interactions; and SDK crash and performance information. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, service reliability and fraud prevention. The app does not request precise location access. See Google’s SDK data disclosures and Google’s privacy policy.

Where required, Google’s consent interface presents applicable choices before ad requests are allowed. Settings includes “Ad privacy choices” when Google requires a privacy-options entry point. Personalization depends on your region, choices and platform permissions. Freezeline currently does not request iOS App Tracking Transparency authorization. A tracking usage description by itself does not grant tracking permission.

You can decline an optional rewarded ad and continue the game without its extra reward. An unavailable or dismissed ad does not erase progress. No purchase or subscription is required to play.

Sharing, feedback and reminders

You decide whether to share a virtual photograph, clip, recap or challenge code through the system share sheet. Shared material may include the titles, character names, puzzle layout or route you chose. The destination app’s privacy practices then apply. Freezeline has no developer-hosted public puzzle feed. Challenge links contain the puzzle code; the landing page does not upload it or use analytics.

Feedback prepares an email draft for you to review. If you send it, we receive the note, email details and app version you include, and use them to respond and resolve the issue. Progress, photographs and account identifiers are not attached automatically. Support email is retained as needed to handle the request; contact us if you want it deleted.

Daily reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device. The game’s camera is a virtual park camera: it does not request access to your physical camera or microphone. Saving or sharing artwork is an action you choose through the system interface.

Control and deletion

  • Settings → Reset progress clears local gameplay progress, the photo collection and recent planned paths. Puzzle Lab creations can be managed separately in the Lab.
  • Deleting the app removes its device-local data. Apple device backups may retain copies under your Apple settings.
  • Local reset does not erase iCloud snapshots or achievements already recorded by Game Center. Manage Apple-held data through Apple’s iCloud and account controls.
  • Use the available ad privacy controls and your Apple privacy settings to manage applicable advertising choices. Google controls the retention of information processed by its services.

Players aged 13 and over

Freezeline is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe you have sent us personal information about a child under 13, contact us so we can address the request.
