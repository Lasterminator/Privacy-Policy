Privacy Policy

Infinite Labyrinth

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Trinath Sai Subhash Reddy Pittala ("we", "us", or "our") built Infinite Labyrinth, a free maze game paid for by ads. This policy explains what the game stores, what it shares, and how to delete it.

1. In short

Infinite Labyrinth keeps your progress on your device and, if you use them, in your Google Play Games or Apple iCloud account so it follows you to another device. It shows full-screen ads through Google AdMob; those ads are personalised only if you agree. It has no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting, and we run no servers of our own.

2. What the game stores on your device

Using the device's app storage, the game keeps:

  • Your progress: Journey stars, gems and power-ups, Daily Maze results, challenges, streaks and badges, and an Endless run you saved to continue later
  • How many free rewards you have taken from ads today, so the daily limits hold
  • Your Endless high scores (if you were signed in to Play Games or Game Center, each entry also notes your player name)
  • Your settings (music, sound, vibration, run style) and whether you have seen the tutorial
  • A random number the game creates on first launch, used only to keep your gem and power-up counts right across devices

Google's consent tool (section 3) also keeps your ad choices on the device. None of this is sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game deletes it from the device.

3. Ads

The game is free because of ads, all of them full-screen and none of them on the maze or the menus: an ad may play when you move on from a results screen after a couple of cleared levels (never during a level), and you can choose to watch an ad for a reward, such as free gems, a free power-up, double gems after a level, or one more heart in Endless. Ads are shown by Google AdMob and are limited to content rated up to Teen.

Where the law asks for consent (the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and some US states), Google's consent form asks you when the game first opens. On iPhone and iPad, Apple then asks whether the game may use your device's advertising identifier. Ads are personalised only if you agree; if you don't, you still get the whole game, with ads that are not based on your interests. Where it applies, Settings has Ad Privacy Choices to change your answer at any time, and you can also reset or turn off the advertising identifier in your device's settings.

To show and count ads, AdMob may receive information about the device and your use of the ads: for example the device model, operating system and language, your IP address and a coarse location derived from it, how you interact with the ads, and, if you allowed it, the device's advertising identifier. Google handles this under Google's Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services). We do not receive it.

4. Google Play Games (optional, Android)

On Android the game can use Google Play Games. On a phone where Play Games is already set up it may sign in by itself when the game starts; otherwise you can sign in from Settings. Signing in is never needed: every part of the game works without it. If you sign in:

  • Leaderboards: your Endless score and your Daily Maze times (labelled with the day) are sent to the Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see them next to your Play Games name.
  • Achievements: the achievements you unlock, and your progress toward them (such as stars earned), are sent to Play Games and shown on your Play Games profile.
  • Cloud saves: a copy of your progress and of a saved Endless run is kept in Play Games Saved Games, so it can be restored on another device.
  • Your profile: the game receives your Play Games display name and player ID, shows your name in the game, and uses the ID only to keep your scores together.

This data is held by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can remove it at any time in the Google Play Games app (Settings, Delete Play Games account and data).

5. Game Center and iCloud (iPhone and iPad)

On iPhone and iPad the game can use Apple's Game Center instead of Google Play Games. A player already signed in to Game Center is signed in when the game starts; otherwise you can sign in from Settings, and the game works fully without it. If you sign in, your Endless score, your Daily Maze times and your achievements are sent to Game Center, where other players can see your scores next to your Game Center name, and the game receives your Game Center display name and player ID for the same uses as on Android.

If your device is signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of your progress and of a saved Endless run in your iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage), so it syncs across your Apple devices. No Game Center sign-in is needed for this. Game Center and iCloud data is held by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center, or turn off iCloud for the game, in the Settings app.

6. What the game does not do

  • No purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no servers of our own
  • No chat, no messages, no user-made content, and no way to contact other players
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera
  • We never sell your data, and we never receive what the ad network collects

7. Children

Infinite Labyrinth is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone; the ads and the optional sign-ins work as described above.

8. Older versions (before 2.5)

Versions of the game before 2.5 used Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) for their leaderboard instead of Google Play Games, storing scores with an anonymous identifier generated by the app. To have that data deleted, see our Request data deletion page.

9. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Data on your device: clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.
  • Ads: change your answer in Settings, Ad Privacy Choices (where it applies), or reset or turn off the advertising identifier in your device's settings.
  • Play Games data (scores, achievements and cloud saves): delete it in the Google Play Games app, or never sign in.
  • Game Center and iCloud data: sign out of Game Center, or turn off or delete the game's iCloud data, in the Settings app.
  • Anything else, or questions: email us (below), and see the Request data deletion page.

For the app's Terms of Service, see our Terms of Service page.
