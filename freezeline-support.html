A little help from the studio.

Freezeline is a photo-finish puzzle game for iPhone. Plan a walk while a camera records the park one column at a time.

Find your next idea

  • Play: lessons, the daily photo, studio remixes, character stories and puzzle collections.
  • Create: a free camera, a live camera and Puzzle Lab for making challenges.
  • Collection: your photographs, winning paths, exhibition wall and discoveries.

Stuck on a photo?

Tap a photo goal to inspect exactly what the camera recorded. Free hints explain the idea. After a miss, “Help with this idea” offers a smaller lesson. The timeline magnifier and tick buttons help place an action precisely.

Your progress

The game saves on your iPhone. In Settings → Protect your progress, create an iCloud backup or a separate local checkpoint. iCloud backup requires Game Center and iCloud Drive. Restoring shows a preview and keeps a recovery copy first. Deleting the app removes device-local data; make an iCloud backup first if you want to keep your photographs.

A friend sent a challenge

Install the game, return to the challenge link, and tap Open. Or paste the code into Create → Puzzle Lab → Enter code. The challenge remains in the link; this website does not store it.

Controls and comfort

Settings includes System, Light and Dark themes, larger left- or right-thumb controls, reduced motion, sound, music and haptics. The game also respects iPhone’s Reduce Motion setting.

Ads

Some builds offer optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between completed practice puzzles. Ads do not appear during a camera sweep. Available privacy choices appear in Settings when required. TestFlight uses test ads.

Contact

Email Freezeline support. Include the app version and what happened. Please avoid sending passwords or sensitive account details. The in-game Feedback screen prepares an email for you to review.
